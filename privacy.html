Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

PocketDox is an iPhone app for storing and organising personal documents. This policy describes how the app handles information. Please review it alongside Apple's iCloud privacy information when you enable iCloud features.

Information you add

You may choose to save PDFs, images, scanned documents, document titles, notes, expiry dates, categories, and people associations. The app uses this information to provide document management, search, and optional reminders.

Local storage and protection

PocketDox stores document originals in app-managed storage on your device, using Apple's file protection mechanisms. Device security and backups also depend on your Apple settings and practices. PocketDox is not a substitute for an independent backup of critical records.

Optional iCloud synchronization

If you enable iCloud synchronization, PocketDox uses Apple's CloudKit service to synchronize app data and document originals through your Apple Account. Apple handles the underlying iCloud infrastructure. Synchronization is primarily triggered when the app launches, returns to the foreground, or is active; background delivery is not guaranteed.

Camera, photos, and files

Camera scanning and document selection are initiated by you. Photos and Files access occurs through Apple's system interfaces for content you choose to import.

Notifications

If you enable expiry reminders and grant permission, PocketDox may schedule notifications on your device. Reminder notifications are optional and can be managed in app and device settings.

Accounts and advertising

PocketDox does not require a separate account or PocketDox username and password. The app's core document-management functionality is not dependent on an advertising account.

Data deletion

You can move documents to Recently Deleted and restore them while available. Permanent deletion removes documents from the app's managed records and is intended to propagate through optional iCloud sync. Cloud and device processing may take time. Independently saved copies, backups, or shared exports are outside the app's deletion controls.

Support requests

If you open a public GitHub support issue, any details you choose to post there are publicly visible and processed by GitHub. Do not include sensitive documents, account credentials, or identifying information in public issues.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as PocketDox changes. The current version will be published at this address, with its revision date shown above.

Contact

For general product support or privacy questions, please use the PocketDox support repository without posting private information. A private contact method is being established; do not submit sensitive documents via public issues.

Important: This page describes the current intended app behavior and should be reviewed against the final distributed build before App Store submission.
