POCKETDOX · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

PocketDox is an iPhone app for storing and organising personal documents. This policy explains how the app handles your information and your choices when using optional iCloud features.

Information you add

You can choose to store PDFs, images, scanned documents, document titles, notes, expiry dates, categories, and associations with people. PocketDox uses this information to provide document management, search, and optional reminders.

Local storage and protection

PocketDox stores document originals in app-managed storage on your device and uses Apple's file protection mechanisms. Device security and backups also depend on your Apple settings. Keep an independent backup of critical documents.

Optional iCloud synchronization

If you enable iCloud sync, PocketDox uses Apple's CloudKit service and your Apple Account to synchronize app information and documents. Apple operates the underlying iCloud infrastructure. PocketDox typically checks for changes when it launches, returns to the foreground, or is active; background delivery is not guaranteed in version 1.0.

Camera, photos, and files

You choose when to scan or import documents. Camera scanning and selection from Photos or Files use Apple's native system interfaces.

Notifications

If you opt in to expiry reminders and allow notifications, PocketDox may schedule reminders on your device. You can manage notification permission in iPhone Settings.

Accounts and advertising

PocketDox doesn't require a separate account, username, or password. Its core document features do not require an advertising account.

Deleting documents

You can restore documents from Recently Deleted while they remain available there. Permanent deletion removes items from app-managed records and is intended to sync with iCloud if enabled. Deletion across devices and cloud services may take time. Copies you exported or backed up separately are not controlled by PocketDox.

Support requests

If you submit a public GitHub support issue, your message is visible to others and processed by GitHub. Do not include sensitive documents or personal data in public issues.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change as PocketDox evolves. Updates will appear here with a revised date.

Contact

For general support or privacy questions that don't require sharing personal information, use the PocketDox support page. Do not post confidential information to public GitHub issues.
