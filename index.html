How can we help?

Quick answers to the questions that matter most.

POCKETDOX · iOS
Getting startedDo I need an account?

No PocketDox sign-in or registration is required. Complete the in-app setup to start organising documents.

Adding documentsHow do I add documents?

Import a PDF or image from Files or Photos, or scan a paper document using your iPhone camera.

OrganisingHow do I organise my records?

Create people and categories, associate documents with them, and use search to find saved records.

iCloudHow does iCloud sync work?

iCloud sync is optional and uses your Apple Account. Changes are checked when the app opens or is active, so updates may take time to appear on other devices. Version 1.0 does not include background push sync.

RecoveryCan I restore a deleted document?

Open Recently Deleted and choose Restore if the document is still there. Documents that have been permanently deleted cannot be recovered using Recently Deleted.

Moving devicesWhat should I do before changing phones?

Keep an independent backup of important documents. If using iCloud sync, confirm the documents have arrived on the new device before erasing the old one.
