HELP CENTER

Frequently asked questions

A few things to know about PocketDox.

Do I need to create an account?

No. PocketDox doesn't require a separate account or sign-in. Just complete the setup in the app to get started.

How do I add a document?

Tap Add Document to scan using your iPhone camera or import a PDF or image from Files or Photos.

How can I organise and find documents?

Group documents by person and category, then use PocketDox search when you need to find something quickly.

How does iCloud sync work?

iCloud sync is optional and uses your existing Apple Account. PocketDox checks for changes when you open or use the app. Changes might not appear immediately on another device; version 1.0 does not support background push syncing.

Can I recover a deleted document?

You can restore an item from Recently Deleted while it's still available there. A permanently deleted document can't be restored from Recently Deleted.

What should I do before switching iPhones?

Keep a separate backup of important originals. If you're using iCloud sync, confirm your documents are available on the new device before erasing the old one.
