GETTING STARTED

Answers to common questions

01

Do I need an account?

No PocketDox sign-in or registration is required. Complete the in-app setup to start organising documents.

02

How do I add documents?

Use Add Document to import a PDF or image from Files or Photos, or scan paper documents with your iPhone camera.

03

How do I organise records?

Create people and categories, then associate documents with them. Use search to find saved records.

04

How does iCloud sync work?

iCloud sync is optional and uses your Apple Account. PocketDox checks for changes when opened and while active; changes may not appear immediately on other devices. Background push sync is not included in version 1.0.

05

How do I restore a deleted document?

Open Recently Deleted and choose Restore, if the document is still available there. Permanently deleted documents cannot be restored through that feature.

06

What if I change phones?

Before replacing or resetting a device, ensure you have independent backups of important documents. If you use iCloud sync, verify documents have arrived on your other device before relying on them.
